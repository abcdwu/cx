# University emblem sources

These local images identify the institutions in the Education section of Chao Xu's personal profile. Their colors and proportions are preserved. They are institutional marks, not original Chao brand artwork.

| Institution | Official reference | Downloaded image mirror |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UCLA | https://brand.ucla.edu/identity/logos-and-marks/seals | https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zhoudaxia233/UniLogo/main/images/imgs0617/University%20of%20California%2C%20Los%20Angeles.png |
| Fudan University | https://www.fudan.edu.cn/bsxt/list.htm | https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zhoudaxia233/UniLogo/main/images/imgs0617/Fudan%20University.png |
| Jiangnan University | https://www.jiangnan.edu.cn/info/1906/11171.htm | https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zhoudaxia233/UniLogo/main/images/imgs0617/Jiangnan%20University.png |

The UCLA image is the Los Angeles campus seal with UCLA beneath the book. The Fudan emblem includes the seal-script name and 1905. The Jiangnan emblem includes the sail and waves described by the university. Losslessly encoded WebP files are served from this repository so page rendering does not rely on a third-party image host.
